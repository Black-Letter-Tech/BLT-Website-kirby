# Homepage refresh

## Goal

Refresh the Black Letter Tech homepage with the strongest material from the
marketing draft while preserving the site's distinctive visual identity.
Position digital forensics and eDiscovery as equal, integrated capabilities.
Use forensics as an accessible conversational entry point without making
eDiscovery appear secondary.

## Decisions

- Position digital forensics and eDiscovery equally. Forensics can open the
  conversation because it is concrete and easy to explain, but eDiscovery must
  not look like an afterthought.
- Emphasize continuity under one roof, from the first device through final
  production.
- Keep the exact term `eDiscovery` prominent for category recognition and SEO.
- Use `Digital Forensics + eDiscovery` near the main headline as the current
  category-line candidate.
- Use `Digital evidence, handled.` as the current headline candidate.
- Preserve the existing visual identity while reviewing the new copy.
- Keep the Black Letter Tech wordmark fixed in the upper-left navigation.
- Remove the animated line that currently establishes the company name. No
  replacement animation is required.
